Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NailDrip ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application ("the App") and related services. By using NailDrip, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Account Information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials through Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In. We do not store your password — authentication is handled by Apple or Google.

User Content. We collect nail designs you create, save, and share within the App, including any customizations, color selections, and design preferences.

Communications. If you contact us for support, we collect your email address and the content of your message.

Waitlist & Marketing. If you sign up for our waitlist or marketing communications, we collect your email address.

Information Collected Automatically

Usage Data. We collect information about how you interact with the App, including features used, designs created, screens viewed, session duration, and actions taken. We use PostHog for product analytics.

Device Information. We collect device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and general diagnostic data.

Crash & Performance Data. We use Sentry to collect crash reports and performance metrics to improve App stability.

Attribution Data. We may use Branch.io or similar services to understand how you discovered the App, including referral links.

Information from Third Parties

Payment Information. Subscriptions are processed through Apple In-App Purchase. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details. Apple handles all payment processing. We receive confirmation of your subscription status and transaction history.

Authentication Providers. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, email address, and a unique identifier. We do not receive your password.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Process your subscription and manage your account
  • Save and sync your nail designs
  • Send you updates, marketing communications, and product announcements (with your consent)
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features and user experience
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Respond to your support requests

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these circumstances:

Service Providers. We share data with third-party services that help us operate the App, including PostHog (analytics), Sentry (crash reporting), Branch.io (attribution), RevenueCat (subscription management), and Apple (payments). These providers are contractually obligated to use your data only to provide services to us.

Legal Requirements. We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of NailDrip, our users, or the public.

Business Transfers. If NailDrip is acquired, merged, or sells assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

AI Features

NailDrip may use artificial intelligence to assist with design suggestions, rendering, and other features. When AI features are active:

  • Your design inputs may be processed by AI models to generate or refine designs
  • We do not use your personal designs to train AI models without your explicit consent
  • AI-generated content is labeled as such within the App
  • You can report AI-generated content that is inappropriate or harmful

Data Retention

We retain your account information and saved designs for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes.

Usage and analytics data is retained in aggregate, de-identified form and is not linked back to your account after deletion.

Your Rights and Choices

All Users

  • Access and Portability. You can request a copy of your personal data by contacting us at support@naildrip.co.
  • Correction. You can update your account information within the App.
  • Deletion. You can delete your account directly within the App. This will permanently remove your saved designs and personal data within 30 days.
  • Marketing Opt-Out. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your data, opt out of the sale of your data (we do not sell your data), and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise your rights, contact us at support@naildrip.co.

European Residents (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights including: the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and objection. Our legal basis for processing is your consent (for marketing), contract performance (for providing the service), and legitimate interests (for analytics and security). To exercise your rights, contact us at support@naildrip.co.

Children's Privacy

NailDrip is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@naildrip.co.

Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy in the App or sending you an email. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, contact us at:

Email: support@naildrip.co
