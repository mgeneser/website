About NailDrip

Why NailDrip?

Your nail inspo is buried in screenshots, scattered across Pinterest boards, and lost in Instagram saves. There's nowhere to actually design a full set, share it, or bring it to your next appointment. So we're making it.

How it works

Design full nail sets from scratch — pick your colors, add patterns, customize each nail. Browse what the community is creating and remix anything that catches your eye. Every remix gives credit to the original artist, so inspiration keeps flowing and creators get love.

What we stand for

Be kind, give credit, celebrate creativity. NailDrip is a space where your ideas build on each other. One design sparks a hundred more — and every creator in the chain gets recognized for it.

Where we're at

We're building in the open, starting with iOS. Early days, big plans. Want in from the start? Join the waitlist.

Got ideas, questions, or feedback? We're at support@naildrip.co.
